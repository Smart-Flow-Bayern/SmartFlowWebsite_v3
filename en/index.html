Common Questions

FAQ

How long does a typical project take?

Project duration depends on scope. An initial process analysis typically takes 2–4 weeks. Larger digitization or automation projects are planned together with you – usually 6 to 16 weeks.

What company sizes is SmartFlow suitable for?

We specialize in small and medium-sized businesses – from tradespeople with 3 employees to regional companies. Our solutions are specifically designed and affordable for this target group.

What does a collaboration with SmartFlow cost?

The initial consultation is free and non-binding. In 30 minutes, we can estimate the effort involved and the realistic ROI you can expect.

Do we need to switch our existing software?

Not necessarily. We first analyze whether your existing tools can be used more effectively. Often, better configuration or integrations can achieve major improvements without introducing entirely new software.

What happens in an initial consultation?

In the free initial consultation (approx. 30 minutes, by phone or video), we get to know your situation: Which processes are currently problematic? What are your goals? Then we assess whether and how we can help.
