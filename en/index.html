Common questions

How long does a typical project take?

It depends entirely on the scope. An initial process analysis usually takes 2 to 4 weeks. We plan larger digitization or automation projects together with you, and most of the time we're looking at something in the range of 6 to 16 weeks.

What company sizes is SmartFlow suitable for?

We specialize in exactly these small and mid-sized businesses, from a tradesperson with 3 employees to an established regional company. Our solutions are tailored precisely to this size and stay affordable.

What does a collaboration with SmartFlow cost?

The initial consultation is free and non-binding. In 30 minutes, we can estimate the effort involved and the realistic ROI you can expect.

Do we need to switch our existing software?

Not necessarily. We first analyze whether your existing tools can be used more effectively. Often, better configuration or integrations can achieve major improvements without introducing entirely new software.

What happens in an initial consultation?

Relaxed and with no sales pressure. In about 30 minutes (by phone or video) you simply tell me where the shoe pinches right now and what you'd like to achieve. I listen, ask a few focused questions, and then tell you honestly whether and how I can help. You leave the conversation with a clear assessment, whether or not we end up working together afterwards.

I don't have time for a project like this — is it still worth it?

Especially then. We plan the rollout so your day-to-day business keeps running: we handle preparation and implementation, you make the decisions. Your time investment is predictable and manageable — and the time sinks slowing you down today are gone afterwards.

We already have a website and a few tools — do we still need this?

A website and individual tools are the start. What matters is whether they work together: if website inquiries are copied into Excel by hand or appointments are still arranged over the phone, you're giving away most of the benefit. Those connections are exactly what we build.
